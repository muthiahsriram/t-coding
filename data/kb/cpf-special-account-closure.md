# What changed

From January 2025 the Special Account is closed once a member turns 55. This replaces the earlier position, in which members aged 55 and above could keep a Special Account balance earning the long-term floor rate while also holding a Retirement Account.

On closure, Special Account savings are transferred to the Retirement Account up to the Full Retirement Sum (FRS). Any remainder is transferred to the Ordinary Account, where it earns the Ordinary Account interest rate rather than the long-term rate.

# Why it matters for advice

The change removes an arbitrage that some members relied on: holding savings in the Special Account above the Full Retirement Sum to earn 4% while remaining withdrawable on short notice. Savings moved to the Ordinary Account under the new rule earn 2.5%, a reduction of 1.5 percentage points on the affected balance.

Members approaching 55 who wish to retain the higher rate on savings above the Full Retirement Sum may top up the Retirement Account to the Enhanced Retirement Sum. Amounts in the Retirement Account are not withdrawable as a lump sum and fund CPF LIFE payouts instead, so this converts liquidity into a higher guaranteed rate and a larger lifelong payout.

# Advice guidance

Any recommendation to a client aged 50 or above that assumes Special Account savings will persist past 55 is incorrect and must be revised. Cash-flow projections that model a 4% return on Special Account balances after 55 overstate retirement income.

Members below 55 are unaffected. Voluntary transfers from the Ordinary Account to the Special Account before 55 remain permitted, remain irreversible, and remain subject to the Full Retirement Sum cap.
