# Retaining Special Account savings after 55

This note described how a member turning 55 could retain Special Account savings above the Full Retirement Sum, so that those savings continued to earn the long-term floor rate of 4.0% per annum rather than the Ordinary Account rate of 2.5%.

The approach relied on the Retirement Account being funded from the Special Account first. By temporarily moving Special Account savings into an approved CPF Investment Scheme instrument shortly before the member's 55th birthday, the Retirement Account would instead be funded from the Ordinary Account, and the invested Special Account savings could be returned afterwards.

Advisers were instructed to raise this with every client in the year before they turned 55, and to document the client's instruction in the file.

# Status

This note is withdrawn and must not be relied on. The Special Account is closed at age 55 from January 2025 and the described sequence no longer produces the stated outcome. Refer to the current guidance on Special Account closure.

Recommendations issued on the basis of this note before its withdrawal should be reviewed at the client's next scheduled meeting, and any projection assuming a post-55 Special Account balance should be reissued.
