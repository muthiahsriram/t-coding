# CPF accounts

Every working Singapore Citizen and Permanent Resident holds three CPF accounts while employed. The Ordinary Account (OA) may be used for housing, insurance, approved investments and education. The Special Account (SA) is for retirement savings and retirement-related investment products. The MediSave Account (MA) is reserved for approved medical expenses and medical insurance premiums, including MediShield Life and Integrated Shield plan premiums.

At age 55 a Retirement Account (RA) is created. Savings are transferred into it from the Special Account first and then the Ordinary Account, up to the Full Retirement Sum. The Retirement Account is what funds CPF LIFE payouts from the payout eligibility age.

# Contribution rates

For employees aged 55 and below, the employer contributes 17% of Ordinary Wages and the employee contributes 20%, for a total of 37%. Contribution rates step down in bands above age 55 and reach 12.5% in total for employees above age 70.

The Ordinary Wage ceiling is the maximum monthly wage attracting CPF contributions. It is being raised in stages and reaches S$8,000 per month from 1 January 2026. The Annual Salary Ceiling, which caps total CPF contributions on both Ordinary and Additional Wages in a calendar year, is S$102,000.

Allocation across the three accounts varies with age. Younger members receive proportionally more into the Ordinary Account; the allocation shifts toward the Special and MediSave Accounts as a member ages.

# Interest rates

The Ordinary Account earns a legislated floor of 2.5% per annum. The Special, MediSave and Retirement Accounts earn a floor of 4.0% per annum. Both floors are reviewed but have not been reduced below these levels.

Members earn an extra 1% per annum on the first S$60,000 of combined CPF balances, of which no more than S$20,000 may come from the Ordinary Account. Members aged 55 and above earn a further additional 1% on the first S$30,000 of combined balances, again with the S$20,000 Ordinary Account sub-limit. A member aged 55 or older can therefore earn up to 6% per annum on part of their retirement savings.

Interest is computed monthly and credited annually. Because the Ordinary Account floor is 2.5% and the Special Account floor is 4.0%, a transfer from OA to SA is irreversible but raises the guaranteed rate on the transferred amount by 1.5 percentage points.

# Retirement sums

Three retirement sums apply at age 55: the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS), the Full Retirement Sum (FRS), which is twice the BRS, and the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS), which is four times the BRS from 2025. A member who owns property and has set aside the Basic Retirement Sum, with a property charge or pledge in place, may withdraw the balance above it.

The Basic Healthcare Sum (BHS) caps the MediSave Account. Amounts above the cap overflow into the Special Account before age 55 and the Retirement Account after, subject to the Full Retirement Sum.
