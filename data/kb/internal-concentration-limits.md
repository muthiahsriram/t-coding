# Single-name limits

No single equity position may exceed 15% of a client's invested portfolio. A position between 10% and 15% is reported to the client at each review with a note on the concentration. A position above 15% is a breach and must be raised with a specific reduction proposal.

The limit applies to look-through exposure. Two Singapore bank holdings totalling 22% of the portfolio are treated as a sector concentration even though neither breaches the single-name limit individually.

# Sector and geography

No single sector may exceed 30% of invested assets, and no single country other than the client's home market may exceed 40%. Singapore exposure is permitted up to 50% in recognition of home bias, currency matching against SGD liabilities and CPF-linked holdings, but home bias above 50% must be justified in writing at the review.

Singapore banks and Singapore REITs are correlated with each other and with the local property cycle. A portfolio holding both is more concentrated than its line-item count suggests, and the review must say so.

# Currency

Unhedged foreign currency exposure is permitted without limit for goals more than seven years away. For any goal inside three years, the funding assets must be held in the currency of the liability, which for almost all clients is SGD. A client saving for a Singapore property purchase in two years should not be funding it from an unhedged USD equity position.

# Liquidity

Assets funding a goal inside twelve months must be held in cash or instruments settling within five business days. CPF and SRS balances are not liquid for this purpose regardless of their composition, because withdrawal is restricted by scheme rules rather than by market conditions.

An emergency reserve of six months of expenses must exist in cash before any surplus is directed to investment. Where the reserve is short, rebuilding it takes priority over every other recommendation in the review.

# Cash drag

Uninvested cash above the emergency reserve and known near-term commitments is a finding, not a neutral position. Cash above 15% of investable assets with no stated purpose must be raised at the review with a specific deployment proposal or an explicit client instruction to hold.

This applies with particular force to SRS cash, which is locked until retirement age and therefore has the longest horizon and the least excuse for sitting uninvested.
